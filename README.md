# Happy Birthday Naila ❤️🎂

A romantic, interactive birthday website created with love for **Naila** by **Muhammad Fajar Andikha**.

## ✨ Features
- 💌 **Wax Sealed Romantic Envelope Opening**: Interactive 3D envelope with custom wax seal that opens to reveal the birthday surprises.
- 🎵 **Built-in Romantic Ambient Music**: Uses the Web Audio API to play gentle, dreamy romantic chords on demand without failing or missing assets.
- 🕯️ **Interactive Birthday Cake & Candle Blowing**: 3-tiered birthday cake where Naila can make a wish and blow out the candles, accompanied by smoke, starry fireworks, and confetti.
- 📜 **Personalized Romantic Love Letter**: Elegant parchment paper with sincere, heartfelt words.
- 💖 **"Reasons I Adore You" Interactive Cards**: 3D flip cards sharing sweet little things.
- 📸 **Polaroid Keepsake Gallery**: Aesthetic snapshots of sweet memories with realistic paper textures and angles.
- 🌸 **"Open When..." Love Capsules**: Modals for moments when she misses you, feels stressed, needs a smile, or wonders how much she is loved.
- 🏺 **Interactive Love Message Jar**: Tap to draw random sweet romantic notes and promises.
- 🌸 **Falling Rose Petals & Particle Engine**: 60fps canvas animation with interactive click hearts anywhere on the screen.
- 📱 **Fully Mobile & Responsive**: Optimized for phones, tablets, and desktop displays.
